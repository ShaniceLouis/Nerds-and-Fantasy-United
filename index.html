Fantasy is my superpower

Fiction is a genre few can compare with. Its versatility and popularity has touched the minds of countless people throughout history. It has inspired billions upon billions of people to think big and embrace their creativity. I am among the many individuals who thrive on their creativity. My creativity is fueled by the stellar works of art pioneering fiction for every audience imaginable. It is tempered by my knowledge and passion for creating fresh ideas. I write to make my voice known to others and strengthen the voices of those who are often silenced. As a writer, I aim to continuously refine my style of writing and seek inspiration for all my big ideas.
