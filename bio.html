My Bio

Ever since I was kid, I’ve always had a powerful imagination at my fingertips. I expressed this form of imagination in the form of writing short stories and drawing characters. This fascination was also influenced by the many great works of art of the film industry. From action to science fiction to comedic drama, I drew inspiration from anything I saw as creative and ambitious. My family and friends would always encourage me to expand my imagination by feeding my knowledge every day. My official start as a writer began in my 11th year in high school. My english teacher opened my eyes to the world of literature and my motivation to become a writer took form. After I enrolled in Virginia Tech, I minored in writing. I spent my time there writing the very best literature I had ever made since high school. Two years later, I graduated in 2019. I chose the path of self publishing and my first book came to the public after several months of hard work and diligence. My lifelong goal now is to publish multiple books in my lifetime and inspire others to pursue their passions with the utmost determination.
